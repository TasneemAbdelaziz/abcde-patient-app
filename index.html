About the Hospital

Alamein Model Hospital

Founded in 2002 and elevated to a "Model Hospital" in 2019, we serve the people and visitors of Matrouh Governorate and the North Coast — under the Secretariat of Specialized Medical Centers.

23,597 m²
A modern medical campus with 107 inpatient beds and 5 operating rooms
20+
Specialties — cardiology & cardiac catheterization, cerebral catheterization, stroke care, neurosurgery, plastic surgery, and more
AI-Equipped
MRI and CT imaging powered by artificial intelligence for precise diagnosis
GAHAR
Initial accreditation by the General Authority for Healthcare Accreditation and Regulation

Opening new horizons for medical tourism in Egypt.
